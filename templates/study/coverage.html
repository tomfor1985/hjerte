Build the inventory in three steps

  1. Map the source. Extract learning points from a complete section; a separate model checks the original text for accuracy and omissions.
  2. Match shared objectives. Combine equivalent concepts across documents while retaining their sources.
  3. Link existing questions. Match the questions already in your bank to the verified objectives.

Each step is saved and reused. Verified objectives can be used after matching and question linking, while unresolved source content remains visible.
